Our new cavity filter seems to work well.
For those of you who want to build it, below are the dimensions. After failed attempts at trying to solder wires on the end rods, we removed part of the wall so that the sma pins directly goes into the end rods.

Enclosure (internal dimensions):
Height: 3.00 cm
Width: 5.80 cm
Length: 16.42 cm

Resonator Rods:
Diameter: 3/8 inch
End resonators (1st and 5th): 4.88 cm long
Interior resonators (2nd, 3rd, 4th): 4.63 cm long

Resonator Positions (from left inner wall):
They are symmetrical about the center of the enclosure.
1.50 cm, 4.71 cm, 8.21 cm, 11.71 cm, 14.92 cm

Tap Point:
1.22 cm from the adjacent end wall

Tuning screws:
#10-32. Inserted 0.5 cm at their optimal tuning position.
Minimum insertion loss requires a smooth surface and good conductors. The skin depth of copper / aluminum at 1.4 GHz is about 2 microns.
